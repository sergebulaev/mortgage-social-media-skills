# Mortgage social media post ideas (120)

Ideas for loan officers and mortgage brokers, grouped by pillar, each with a
first line you can adapt. None states a payment, a down payment amount, a
term offer or your own rate, so none needs a Reg Z disclosure as written; the
writer still checks every post. Numbers come from the source named in the
idea, fetched the day you post.

Contents
- Rates and the market (1-20)
- First-time buyers (21-40)
- Loan types explained (41-60)
- Process, documents and credit (61-80)
- Myths (81-95)
- Realtor and builder partners (96-108)
- Local, people and proof (109-120)
