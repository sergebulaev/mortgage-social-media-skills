# Year calendar for loan officer posts

Dated hooks for planning. Dates fixed by law or by the publisher are marked;
the rest are seasonal habits. Check each year's exact date before posting.

Contents
- Every week
- By month
- Movable and yearly data dates

## Every week

- **Thursday:** Freddie Mac PMMS at 12:00 ET -> the Rate Desk post
  (Wednesday in a Thanksgiving or holiday week).
- **Monday or Tuesday:** an education post (myth, document, process step).
- **Weekend:** open houses with realtor partners (LinkedIn on Friday).

## By month

| Month | Hooks |
|---|---|
| January | New conforming loan limits take effect (Jan 1); "new year, credit check-up"; Form 1098 mortgage interest statements arrive (by Jan 31) |
| February | Tax documents for pre-approval; Valentine's "buying with a partner" (no family-status targeting) |
| March | Spring market starts; pre-approval before open-house season |
| April | Fair Housing Month (April); tax day (April 15): self-employed buyers and tax returns |
| May | Peak buying season; Military Appreciation Month (VA loan education, no government-affiliation wording) |
| June | National Homeownership Month (June); end of school year moves |
| July | Mid-year rate review; July 4 closings and holiday closing timelines |
| August | Back-to-school moves; appraisal and inspection explainers |
| September | Fall market; National Preparedness Month: homeowners insurance and escrow |
| October | Fiscal year start for some programs; "credit freeze before holiday shopping" |
| November | Veterans Day (Nov 11): VA loan facts; FHFA announces next year's limits (late Nov); Thanksgiving: rate post moves to Wednesday |
| December | Year-end closings and holiday closing calendars; tax documents to keep; new limits on Jan 1 |

## Movable and yearly data dates

- FOMC meetings: 8 a year (federalreserve.gov calendar). Post the day after,
  without predictions.
- FHFA conforming loan limits: late November (11/25/2025, 11/26/2024).
- FHA limits: shortly after FHFA.
- Daylight saving changes and federal holidays: office hours posts
  (`tests/fixtures/feeds/holidays-2026-us.json` shows the year's list).
- Local: county property tax deadlines (check your county's site), first-time
  buyer program windows in your state.
