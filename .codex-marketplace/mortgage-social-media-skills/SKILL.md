# Mortgage Social Media Skills

A bundle of 3 skills for mortgage loan officers and mortgage brokers: what to post and when, posts
written in your voice with the Reg Z and NMLS check, a data card for the
picture, and drafts in Publora. One signature move: Thursday Rate Desk.

## When to use this bundle

- **A month of posts, or first-time setup** -> `mortgage-content-planner`
- **One post, a caption, or checking a draft** -> `mortgage-post-writer`
- **This week's rate post (Thursday, Freddie Mac PMMS), the yearly loan-limit post** -> `mortgage-rate-desk`

## Core pattern

1. **Memory first.** `./mortgage-memory/`: profile, voice, rules, facts, ideas,
   lessons, consent (`references/memory.md`).
2. **Facts from sources.** Every number comes from a fetched feed or from
   memory (`references/sources.md`, `lib/provenance.py`).
3. **The check, every time.** Reg Z and NMLS plus the common rules,
   shown with every fix (`references/compliance.md`, `references/precedence.md`).
4. **A draft, not a post.** Publora draft with the card attached; scheduling
   only after a yes to a read-back (`references/publishing.md`).
