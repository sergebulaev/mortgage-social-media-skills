# Voice rules

The owner's voice first (`voice.md`), these rules always. `lib/style.py`
checks the enumerated ones; a STRICT finding is fixed before a draft is shown.

## Strict (fix every time)

1. No em dash (—) and no en dash (–) anywhere in a post. Use a period, a comma
   or a colon.
2. No stock AI openers: "In today's...", "In the ever-changing...", "In a
   world where...", "Let's dive in", "Are you ready to...".
3. No AI vocabulary: delve, leverage, game-changer, unlock the, elevate your,
   seamless, robust, tapestry, testament to, navigate the complex, in the
   realm of, embark, harness the power.
4. No dead closer as the last line: "What do you think?", "Thoughts?", "Let
   us know in the comments", "Drop a comment below", "Agree?". End on the
   useful line or a concrete next step (call, book, stop by).
5. No "It's not just X, it's Y".
6. Within the network's length (Instagram and TikTok 2,200; LinkedIn 3,000;
   Bluesky 300; Mastodon and Threads 500 per part; X 280 per part).

## Soft (prefer)

- Specific beats general: the ZIP, the street, the date, the number from the
  source, the name of the dish or the reading.
- Short sentences, varied length. One idea per post.
- Capitalize names. Write the business's name the way the owner writes it.
- At most 3-5 hashtags, local ones first (#AustinTX), none on LinkedIn unless
  the owner uses them. No emoji runs.
- The first line works alone: it is all most people see.
- Plain words a client uses, not the trade's jargon, unless the audience is
  the trade (a lender's realtor partners on LinkedIn).

## Per network, quickly

| Network | What works for a local business |
|---|---|
| Facebook | the main local network: 40-120 words, a photo or card, a clear next step |
| Instagram | the card or photo carries it; caption first line is the hook; 3-5 hashtags |
| LinkedIn | partners and referral sources; first-person, a takeaway; link in the first comment |
| Google Business Profile | not in Publora: hand the text over in the manual block if the owner uses it |
| X, Threads, Bluesky | short, one number, the source link |
| TikTok, YouTube Shorts | needs video: the skill writes a 20-40 s script and caption, the owner films |
